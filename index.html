About Me

Welcome! I'm Sam, a yoga teacher, a surfer, and an avid baker living in Durham, North Carolina. In my classes, we often build towards a pinnacle pose by breaking it down into its component parts. We'll practice the underlying actions in related poses and work to develop the understanding and strength needed to make and support the peak pose. I love hearing my student's revelations from this approach! A lot of people can do more than they initially think and we have fun doing it! Beyond the physical poses, I encourage patience, self-compassion, being present, and connection between mind, body, and breath. In my experience, learning the alignment and engagement within yoga postures helps to develop greater mobility with consistency. Even if a version of a posture feels out of reach, I truly believe there is something for everyone to work on and benefit from in steps along the way.

Much of my teaching style and knowledge has come from training with exceptional yoga teachers. I completed my 200-hour teacher training in 2016 with In 2016, I completed my RYT-200 yoga teacher training with Sommer and Paul Sobin of Thousand Petals Yoga. Several years later in 2022, I completed my 300-hour advanced teacher training with Christina Sell.

Outside of yoga, I have a Ph.D. in Biomedical Engineering from Duke University and started my own medical device / imaging consulting company, Archer Woods Consulting, LLC. After a few years, I pivoted to a biomedical research analyst, where I honed my skills of analyzing scientific literature and communicating that in public dissemination. Most recently, I've relaunched Archer Woods Consulting to jump back into engineering for medical devices.

Location and Parking

The Durham Friends Meeting House is at 404 Alexander Dr., directly next to the Ronald McDonald house. There is a well-marked large parking lot between the two buildings. Please only park on the right side of the lot. The left side is for Ronald McDonald House staff and guests. To find my class, walk up through that parking lot between the meeting hall and the Ronald McDonald house. You'll see a walkway that that goes up to the lower part of the meeting house building on your right. Take the walkway up to the double doors right in front of you on the ground level. Doors will open at~6:10, and we'll start at 6:15 pm. There is also street parking on Alexander Dr., but pay attention to signage, as there are some 'No Parking' areas.

Not ready to jump into a group class?

If you are new to yoga and want to develop a strong foundation, if you want to use yoga as a compliment to other forms of movement, or if you are a seasoned practicioner who wants to deepen your practice, a private session could be right for you.

In a private class, you'll get:
  • Individualized pose sequences and modifications for new and old injuries
  • Adjustments to help find optimal alignment for your body
  • The ability to work on specific goals
  • The opportunity to develop home practice
